# Design Rules

- Keep the UI as a clinic operations product, not a marketing site.
- Preserve all screen names and core workflows listed in `SCREEN_LIST.md`.
- Keep mobile drawer navigation visible and easy to reach.
- Use placeholder content only.
- Do not include real patient names, phone numbers, MRNs, files, addresses, or identifiers.
- Do not include secrets, API keys, tokens, passwords, `.env` values, uploads, logs, DB files, screenshots, PDFs, spreadsheets, or generated zips.
- Keep clinical content as draft/review language.
- Do not add autonomous diagnosis, prescribing, medication dosing automation, or production readiness claims.
- Do not remove safety notices.
