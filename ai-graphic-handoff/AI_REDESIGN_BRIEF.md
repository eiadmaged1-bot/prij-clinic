# AI Redesign Brief

Goal: upgrade the visual language of Prij Clinic while preserving product intent, safety boundaries, and clinical governance.

Audience: OB/GYN and women's health clinic staff, including owner, doctor, receptionist, nurse, accountant, and admin roles.

Design direction:
- Calm premium clinical workspace.
- Dense enough for repeated clinic work.
- Touch-friendly on tablets and phones.
- Clear role and review states.
- No decorative clinical claims.

Required safety tone:
- AI is assistive only.
- Clinical drafts require doctor review.
- Prescription and medication areas must not imply automated dosing, diagnosis, or prescribing.
- Real clinical record changes require audit logs in the app; this static package is visual only.

Do not redesign this into a marketing landing page. The first screen should remain an operational dashboard.
