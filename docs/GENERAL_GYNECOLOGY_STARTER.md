# General Gynecology Starter

The Gynecology tab in the patient workspace is a recording-only clinician workspace.

General visit fields:
- reason for visit
- menstrual history
- bleeding pattern
- pelvic pain symptoms
- discharge or infection symptoms
- contraception history
- relevant medical/surgical history
- exam notes
- doctor impression
- doctor plan
- follow-up date

Starter templates:
- AUB starter
- Pelvic pain starter
- PCOS starter
- Fibroid/ovarian cyst starter
- Contraception starter

Safety boundaries:
- No automatic diagnosis
- No treatment suggestion
- No medication recommendation
- No contraceptive eligibility automation
- No automatic prescribing
- No automatic dosing
- Doctor impression and plan are written manually by the doctor.

Saved gynecology records are audit-backed through the existing gynecology visit workflow and are intended for fake/synthetic local demo data until production readiness is completed.
