# Ultrasound Calculator Governance

Ultrasound-related formulas are intentionally conservative.

## Current Behavior

- OB ultrasound records can store raw measurements.
- Ultrasound GA on scan date can be used as a dating candidate when GA is entered directly.
- CRL, BPD, HC, AC, FL, Hadlock EFW, fetal growth percentile, Doppler percentile, AFI, and SDP formula entries are draft/catalog-only.

## Disabled Until Verified

- No raw biometry-to-GA calculation.
- No EFW calculation from unverified coefficients.
- No percentile interpretation.
- No fetal growth restriction diagnosis.
- No Doppler interpretation.
- No fetal image interpretation.

Raw measurements remain recording-only and require clinician interpretation.
