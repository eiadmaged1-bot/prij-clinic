# Master Feature Backlog

Date: 2026-06-28

This backlog is planning only. V0.1 remains a local/private demo and must not be used with real patient data.

## MVP

- Patient files, registration, search, and patient-centered workspace.
- Appointments, calendar, queue, and check-in.
- Encounters, prescriptions, investigations, reports, pregnancy, and ultrasound records.
- Billing invoices and payment records without a real payment gateway.
- Consent foundation, audit logs, RBAC, scope checks, and security tests.
- Owner Control Center for service pricing, users/roles overview, theme settings, audit review, and safe overrides.
- Disabled/mock-only AI draft review placeholder.

## V1

- Patient-file-native actions for booking, queue check-in, encounter creation, orders, reports, billing, and consent.
- Role-specific dashboards for owner, receptionist, doctor, nurse, and accountant.
- Patient timeline with filtered clinical, billing, consent, and operational events.
- Service catalog integration into invoice line creation.
- Consent workflow improvements with policy-specific consent types and review states.
- Browser-level QA and visual regression screenshots for all themes.

## V2

- Inventory foundation for clinic supplies and ultrasound/report consumables.
- Secure file storage for report attachments after authorization, encryption, malware scanning, audit logging, and legal review.
- Patient portal planning, messaging consent, and notification preferences.
- Insurance workflow planning if required by clinic operations.
- Analytics and operational reporting with PHI-safe aggregation.

## Future

- WhatsApp or messaging bot only after consent, audit, opt-out, and privacy review.
- CRM and patient engagement workflows.
- Advanced analytics, forecasting, and multi-branch optimization.
- External lab/PACS integrations.
- AI-assisted drafts only after provider privacy, consent, audit, RBAC, and doctor-review controls are complete.

## OB/GYN Differentiator

- OB/GYN-first patient file structure.
- Pregnancy and ultrasound workflow tuned for women's health.
- Explicit safety boundary: no automatic diagnosis, no fetal-image diagnostic AI, and physician interpretation required.
