# Dermatology reference

## v1.5.1 governed workspace

The live Dermatology workspace contains 13 source-linked topics: acne, hyperpigmentation, melasma, eczema/dermatitis, psoriasis, fungal infections, rosacea, urticaria, alopecia, hirsutism, intertrigo, vulvar dermatology, and sensitive-area pigmentation. Each topic has navigation, search/reset, structured assessment prompts, red flags, body-area safeguards, investigations, pregnancy/lactation cautions, referral guidance, patient advice, related topics, and visible source/version/review state.

All seeded topic content starts as `needs_clinical_review`. Sources are limited to official NICE guidance, American Academy of Dermatology guideline collections, British Association of Dermatologists patient-information collections, ACOG guidance, and the Endocrine Society hirsutism guideline. Content is concise and paraphrased; source URLs and publication/version metadata are retained.

Approved generic options remain empty until a medicine-level source and clinical review support the connection. The workspace links to the Drug Atlas without inferring treatment from ATC classification. It never diagnoses, selects treatment, prescribes, or changes a patient record automatically.

Sensitive-area content distinguishes external genital skin, mucosa, groin fold, axilla, inner thigh, and facial skin. Pigmentation workflows begin with assessment and red-flag review, not cosmetic treatment assumptions.
