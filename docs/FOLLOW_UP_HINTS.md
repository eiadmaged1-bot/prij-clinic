# Follow-up Hints

Follow-up hints are deterministic reminders only. They do not diagnose, prescribe, rank treatment, or automate decisions.

Current hints cover:
- Intake red flags awaiting doctor review.
- Requested investigations or referrals not reviewed.
- Results received but not reviewed.
- Allergy review during prescription workflows.
- Missing current medication history.
- Previous anemia-related prescription context.
