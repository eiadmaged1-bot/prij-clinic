# EDD Calendar And Dating

v1.3.9 keeps EDD clinical output review-first:

- LMP: EDD = LMP + 280 days.
- Conception/ovulation/IUI: EDD = known conception or ovulation date + 266 days.
- Ultrasound GA: scan date plus remaining days to 40 weeks.
- IVF/ICSI day 3 transfer: transfer date + 263 days.
- IVF/ICSI day 5 transfer: transfer date + 261 days.
- IVF/ICSI day 6 transfer: transfer date + 260 days.
- CRL/raw biometry can be recorded as measurement input; no unvalidated CRL formula is invented.

Multiple candidates can exist. A doctor can set the best obstetric estimate and review/lock EDD. Locked EDD cannot be changed or voided without a reason and audit.

The Calendar page now has two modes: Schedule/Queue Calendar and EDD Clinical Calendar. The EDD month view shows locked or reviewed EDD records only, with patient file links and current pregnancy context.
