# Verified Protocol Pack 3: Contraception Eligibility and Counseling

Status: implemented for local demo and regression testing.

This pack adds eligibility and counseling snapshots for contraception workflows. The app supports clinician review of method considerations but does not choose a method, prescribe, or automate dosing.

Safety boundaries:
- No automatic method selection.
- No automatic prescription.
- No dose schedules.
- Doctor must check pregnancy possibility, contraindications, medical history, thrombotic risk, migraine with aura, hypertension, smoking and age risk, postpartum/lactation status, drug interactions, STI risk, patient preference, and local eligibility guidance.
- Device/procedure topics include general consent and safety checks only.

Verified codes:
CONTRACEPTION_COUNSELING, COMBINED_HORMONAL_CONTRACEPTION, PROGESTIN_ONLY_PILL, INJECTABLE_CONTRACEPTION, CONTRACEPTIVE_IMPLANT, COPPER_IUD, LNG_IUS, EMERGENCY_CONTRACEPTION, POSTPARTUM_CONTRACEPTION, POST_ABORTION_CONTRACEPTION, CONTRACEPTION_WITH_MEDICAL_CONDITIONS, MISSED_PILLS, IUD_INSERTION_COUNSELING, IUD_REMOVAL, IMPLANT_INSERTION_REMOVAL, STERILIZATION_COUNSELING, PRECONCEPTION_COUNSELING.
