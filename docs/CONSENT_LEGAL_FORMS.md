# Consent And Legal Forms

V0.7 adds `ConsentTemplate` and extends `ConsentRecord` for demo signature metadata.

## Current Scope

- Templates are local demo placeholders.
- Demo signature capture is not a real electronic signature.
- Legal review is required before real clinic use.
- AI processing consent is seeded inactive by default.

## Audit

Template create/update, demo signing, and consent review are audited.
