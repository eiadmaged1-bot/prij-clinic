# Reception Workspace

`/reception/today` shows today appointments, walk-in/check-in action, waiting queue, patient search, new patient shortcut, appointment status, payment note if available, doctor handoff, visit reason notes, cancellation/no-show reasons, patient workspace links, and billing links.

Receptionist workflow is limited to reception operations. Receptionist and accountant roles remain blocked from Care Assist evaluation, medication safety profile management, protocol verification, and advanced clinical safety tools.

Use synthetic/local review records only. The workspace is not production-ready.
