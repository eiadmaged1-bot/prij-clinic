# Deleted Workflow Clutter

v1.3.9 removes demo/training workflow blocks from normal clinic UI:

- Open Day Checklist.
- Close Day Checklist.
- Waiting-time alerts.
- Temporary Queue Ticket QR and queue ticket QR printing.
- Guided staff help.
- Copyable message templates.

Permanent Patient QR remains available from patient files and reception QR tools. The real workflow now focuses on current patient, waiting patients, profile/history preview, start or continue visit, prescriptions, investigations, follow-up, billing, and finish.
