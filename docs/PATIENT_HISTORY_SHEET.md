# Patient History Sheet

The patient history sheet is structured documentation for OB/GYN care. It does not diagnose, prescribe, interpret investigations, or replace the doctor.

Sections:
- Chief complaint.
- History of present illness.
- Menstrual history.
- Obstetric history.
- Gynecological history.
- Contraception history.
- Infertility history.
- Past medical history.
- Past surgical/operation history.
- Medication history.
- Allergy history.
- Family history.
- Social history.
- Previous investigations and imaging.
- Notes/free text.

Catalog-linked rows keep both IDs and snapshots so old patient files remain readable after catalog wording changes. All patient-history writes are audit-recorded through the existing audit service.
