# v1.2.1 Responsive Reception Cleanup Lock

v1.2.1 cleans the app after v1.2.0 while preserving the mouse-first doctor productivity work.

- Login is a minimal premium staff sign-in page.
- New Patient uses Full name and Year of birth with calculated age.
- Referral/source is removed from patient creation.
- Sensitive status is stored only through the bottom `Not sexually active` checkbox.
- Receptionist home is a single-purpose queue workflow.
- `مستعجل` is its own visit type and automatically becomes next after the current in-room patient.
- Doctors can preview waiting patient files without starting visits.
- Responsive support covers mobile, tablet, laptop, desktop, and Arabic RTL.

Real patient data remains blocked until final signoff. External AI remains disabled by default. The system must not perform autonomous diagnosis, prescribing, dosing, or treatment ranking.
