# Infertility Workflow

The patient file is permanent. Infertility is a patient type/filter and infertility phases/episodes preserve the medical journey.

The Infertility tab includes:
- Infertility summary and phase context.
- Induction of Ovulation cycles.
- AMH request/result fields.
- Follicular monitoring with right and left ovary follicle fields.
- E2 / estradiol serial results.

Doctor safety:
- Medication notes are manually written.
- Plans are manually written.
- The system does not diagnose, prescribe, dose, rank treatment, or suggest an induction plan.
