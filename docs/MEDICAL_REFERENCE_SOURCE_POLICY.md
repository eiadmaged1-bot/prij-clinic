# Medical Reference Source Policy

Reference sources support clinician review only.

Hard rules:

- No real patient data in import fixtures.
- No autonomous diagnosis.
- No autonomous prescribing.
- No self-medication guidance.
- No pharmacy stock, purchase, checkout, or fulfillment workflow.
- Medication strength/form/package is market metadata only, never patient directions.
- AI clinical output, if added later, remains draft-only until reviewed and approved by a doctor.
- Do not bypass login, membership, copyright, paywall, or licensing restrictions.
- Keep guideline and medication imports auditable with source, license/access status, and verification status.

Source registries may list public, restricted-public, owner-provided, or licensed-provider sources. Actual content import requires policy review before ingestion.
