# Smart Clinical Search

Confirmed assignments retain patient/tag IDs, source type/record, optional source encounter, manual/derived type, actor, doctor confirmation, active/historical/resolved status, effective/resolution dates, notes, and audit history.

Search supports AND, OR, and NOT plus status filtering. Results explain matching tag or medication evidence. Manual doctor assignments are confirmed; derived assignments remain suggestions until a Doctor role confirms them.

Saved cohorts, complete procedure/oncology workflows, and every requested automatic source adapter remain future work.
