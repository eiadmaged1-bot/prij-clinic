# Patient Statement

The patient statement is sourced from patient invoices and payments. It is available from the Billing page and patient Billing tab.

Statement content:

- Invoice list
- Invoice status
- Service lines
- Payments
- Refund/reversal or void visibility where present
- Totals paid and outstanding balance

The statement is print-friendly for local clinic review. It is not a full accounting ledger and does not export PHI unless a future audited export workflow is explicitly added.
