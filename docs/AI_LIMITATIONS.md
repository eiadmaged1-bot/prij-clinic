# AI Limitations

v0.17.0 is not a production AI release.

Limitations:
- AI is assistive only.
- AI drafts require doctor approval.
- No autonomous diagnosis, prescribing, dosing, treatment ranking, or final clinical decision support is included.
- External AI calls are disabled by default.
- Follow-up reminder drafts are not sent automatically.
- Patient-file search is patient-scoped and deterministic.
- The missing-field checklist is administrative/completeness support only, not clinical advice.
- PHI/PII must not be sent externally unless explicit future governance is added.
