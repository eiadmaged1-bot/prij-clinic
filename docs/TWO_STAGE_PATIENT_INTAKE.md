# Two-stage Patient Intake

Stage 1 is secretary intake: administrative details, patient-reported history, vitals, OBS/GYN intake, and red flags.

Stage 2 is doctor clinical note: review of patient-reported intake, examination, ultrasound findings, clinical impression, risk classification, and plan.

Reception can create, update, and submit intake. Doctors can review and sign. Reception cannot write diagnosis, examination, clinical impression, prescription, final risk assessment, or treatment plan.
