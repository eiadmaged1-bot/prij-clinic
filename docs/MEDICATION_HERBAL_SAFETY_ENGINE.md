# Medication Herbal Safety Engine

The safety engine creates draft alerts for clinician review. Alerts remain visible after review or override.

Implemented alert categories:
- Allergy match.
- Possible duplicate therapy.
- Herbal or supplement caution.
- General review completion.

Current status:
- Seeded demo rules are intentionally limited.
- Commercial clinical interaction/dosing databases are not integrated.
- The system does not edit prescriptions or patient instructions.
- Override requires authorized Doctor/Admin/Owner permission and a reason.
